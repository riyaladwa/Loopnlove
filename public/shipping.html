Shipping & Returns Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Shipping Rates & Delivery Times

We provide pan-India delivery. Orders over ₹999 qualify for FREE standard delivery. For orders under ₹999, a flat shipping charge of ₹79 is applied at checkout.

In-stock ready bouquets dispatch within 24–48 hours. Custom orders typically require 3–5 days to hand-craft before dispatch.

2. Packaging

Every bouquet is secured in custom crush-proof corrugated boxes with delicate tissue wrap to ensure your handcrafted piece arrives in immaculate condition.

3. Returns & Replacements

Because each piece is handmade to order, we do not accept general returns. However, if your order arrives damaged during transit, please contact us with photos within 48 hours of delivery at riyaladwa9@gmail.com, and we will gladly arrange a prompt replacement.
